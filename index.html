about us

A place where kids can just be themselves

Just Be You is a licensed preschool and child care in Elk River, Minnesota. It's a small, warm, home-like setting where children are free to be curious, play hard and grow at their own pace.

We love being outside. Searching for monarch caterpillar eggs, carving pumpkins, sorting fall leaves and singing our "It's Friday" song are all part of the fun.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions parents ask most. Can't see yours? Call (763) 360-4341 or send us a message.

What are your hours?

We're open Monday to Friday, 7:00am to 4:30pm.

Do you have any openings?

Yes. We currently have 3 openings for children aged 2 years and up. Spaces are limited, so get in touch soon.

Is Just Be You licensed?

Yes. Just Be You is a licensed preschool and child care in Elk River, Minnesota.

Where are you located?

We're in Elk River, Minnesota. Because we're a small, home-like setting, we share the full address when you book a visit.

Can we visit before enrolling?

Absolutely. Send an enquiry or call (763) 360-4341 to set up a time to come and see us.

Do the children play outside?

Every day we can! We have a big backyard with a play structure and playhouses, and we spend time outside in every season.
